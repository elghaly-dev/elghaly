Privacy policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

Who we are

Iris for Alexa+ ("Iris") is made by elghaly, Dubai, UAE. Questions about this policy: support@elghaly.dev.

The short version

  • Iris is free and read-only. There is no account, no sign-in and no payment.
  • Iris does not store what you ask it. We do not sell or share it.
  • Iris never needs your recovery (seed) phrase or private keys. It refuses text that looks like a recovery phrase.
  • Iris does not use cookies, ads or analytics.

What Iris receives

When you use Iris through Alexa+, Amazon turns what you say into text and sends Iris a short request. The request names one Iris tool and the text that tool needs. That can be:

  • a message you received, or a description of what happened;
  • a link or a domain name;
  • a public Solana transaction signature (or an explorer link that contains one);
  • a safety tip topic;
  • a device type: iPhone, Android or wallet.

Iris does not receive your voice recording. Iris does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, Amazon account details or location, and its code does not read any user or device ID. If a request carries other technical fields, Iris ignores them and does not keep them.

Like any web server, Iris also sees the basic connection details needed to send an answer back, such as the IP address the request came from. For Alexa+ requests, that is normally an Amazon server, not your device.

How Iris uses it

Iris uses the request only to answer that one request. Scam, link and safety checks run on our server with fixed rules. The request is held in memory while it is answered, and is not kept after the answer is sent.

What we store

Nothing about your request. Iris has no database, writes no files about requests, and has no analytics or tracking.

If something goes wrong, the server writes a short error line that names only the type of error. It does not include what you said, the link, the signature or any other text you sent.

Hosting

Iris runs on a cloud hosting service. Our hosting and network providers may keep standard technical logs, such as the IP address, time and address of a request, to run and protect their services, under their own policies. Iris does not add the content of your requests to those logs.

Outside services

  • Amazon Alexa+. Your voice, your Alexa account and your Alexa history are handled by Amazon, under the Amazon Privacy Notice. You can review and delete Alexa voice history in the Alexa Privacy Hub.
  • Solana network. Only when you ask Iris to explain a transaction, our server sends that public transaction signature to a public Solana RPC endpoint (by default, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com) to read the transaction. The request comes from our server, not your device, and contains only the signature. Solana transactions are public by design.
  • Nothing else. Checking a link does not open the link. Iris does not send your text to an AI service, an advertiser or any other third party.

Recovery phrases and keys

Iris never asks for your recovery phrase. If your text looks like a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, Iris refuses it before any check or network call, and does not store or log it. Iris holds no private keys. It cannot connect a wallet, sign, send or approve anything.

Never say or type your recovery phrase to any assistant, app or person. The app or device you typed it into may keep a copy.

The test page (/sim)

Iris also has a simple test page at /sim that sends the same requests as Alexa+. The page sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and loads no analytics or outside scripts.

If you use "hold to talk" on that page, your browser's own speech recognition turns your speech into text. Depending on your browser, the browser maker may process that audio on its servers under its own policy. Iris receives only the resulting text. "Speak it" uses your browser's own voice.

Children

Iris is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Because Iris keeps no request data, we hold nothing about you to show, export or delete. If you have a question or a request, write to support@elghaly.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
