Pillar 1

Keep your secrets safe

Before an assistant does anything for you, you should know what it can see, what it keeps and what it is allowed to do. We go through it on your own accounts, on your screen.

What never to share

Passwords, API keys, recovery phrases, card numbers and other people's private data never go into an AI chat. We show you what to send instead.

Privacy settings

Chat history, memory, shared links and connected apps: we check each one with you and switch off what you do not need.

Training opt-outs

Most AI tools let you stop your chats being used to train their models. We find the setting in the tools you use and turn it off with you.

Permission first

Your assistant gets only the access it needs, and asks you before it sends, buys, posts or deletes anything.

Pillar 2

Your own private assistant

Think of it as hiring: we help you pick the right one, show it how you work, and give it its first real jobs.

  1. Choose the assistant that fits your work and your budget, often Grok Bot. Free plans first; we only suggest a paid plan when it is worth it.
  2. Set it up on your own accounts, with you at the keyboard. You hold every login and every key; we never ask for them.
  3. Train it with clear instructions about your business, your tone and your rules, so it answers like part of your team.
  4. Use it every day for real tasks such as email drafts, research, planning and customer replies, checking its work before anything goes out.
