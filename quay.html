What it is

What QUAY is

QUAY is a set of read-only reviews, reports and alerts for people who run Solana bots. You send public data, such as transaction signatures, a mint address or the feed to watch, and we send back a written answer or an alert.

Nothing of ours touches your config, your wallet or your transactions. Anything that cannot be read from public data is marked UNKNOWN instead of guessed.

Read-only

We read public data. We never touch your config, your wallet or your transactions.

Public data only

Never a key, a seed phrase or an RPC URL with a token in it. We will not ask for or accept one.

UNKNOWN, not guessed

What public data cannot show is marked UNKNOWN, with the reason.

Monthly or one-time

Two monthly plans and three one-time products. Nothing renews by itself.

Who it is for

Is QUAY for you?

  • You run a Solana bot and want to see where its tips and fees go.
  • You want a message when an RPC or gRPC feed you rely on goes quiet.
  • You keep your wallet and keys: we only ever ask for public data.

FAQ

Questions

Do I have to give you keys or wallet access?

No. You send public data only. Never a private key, a seed phrase, or an RPC URL with a token in it: we will not ask for or accept one.

Do the monthly plans renew by themselves?

No. The checkout charges one month and does not renew automatically. Order again for each further month.

What do I send for a Fee review?

Up to 200 transaction signatures (signatures only), your current priority-fee and tip settings, and which venue or aggregator you route through.

How fast is delivery?

The Fee review is one page by email within 48 hours of a complete brief, in English. For the other products, the delivery or start time is confirmed in our reply to your order.

Will following a review lower my costs?

It may not. A review is a recommendation only. Signatures cannot show your fills, your strategy, your RPC latency, or failures that are not recorded on chain, and we make no claim about earnings, returns or savings.

Can I get my money back?

If you want your money back, you get it: no form, no reason and no time limit. Email support@elghaly.dev. See the refund policy.
